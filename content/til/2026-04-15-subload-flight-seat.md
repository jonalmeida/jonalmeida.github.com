If you get stuck at an airport and your flight is rescheduled for a future date, the airline desk at the airport may give you a flight without a confirmed seat (sometimes referred to as a "subject to load" ticket). They mention that you need to take that ticket, head to the gate when it opens, and wait there until everyone else is boarded so that you get an open seat if the flight is full.

This is toss-up because the flight can have empty seats at the time but it fills up until the gate opens, or an already full flight is sometimes what you get when the airline staff _think_ it may open up (spoilers: it doesn't always!)

When you get your sub-load ticket, go online and immediately try to check-in within the 24-hour online check-in period and select a seat, whether it's free or paid. You then have a confirmed seat on the flight!
